Price my listing

Uses your player's actual overall (not the buy-side's overall-1 discount) — this is what similar players are really selling for, so you don't underprice. Shows what you'd net at a few price points after MFL's 5% fee.

Check a deal

Floor price for players your age and one overall point below — a conservative value anchor, not an average. Comps build up over time from live market activity — expect thin data for the first while, especially for uncommon age/overall combos. Sales under $8 are ignored.

Floor price lookup

Search "overall age [position]" — e.g. "75 24 ST", or just "62 25" for all positions. Or look up by player ID. Values use overall minus 1 as the conservative reference (unless the overall is a tier break — 55/65/75/85/95 — where a point down means a different rarity tier entirely, so we don't adjust). Shows the floor at that overall/age, the floor one age-year lower too, the position-specific floor, and the midpoint between the general and position floors. Live = queried fresh from MFL every search; recent sale history = our own tracked data, for context. Retirement flags (player-ID lookups only): yellow/orange/red = retiring in 3/2/1 seasons. Flagged players are valued against other flagged players, not by age. A red flag on a player still under contract makes them worth $0.

Bulk agency valuer

Import an MFL export CSV (pulls player IDs, stats, and your own logged value automatically) — or paste a plain list of IDs, one per line. Fast mode uses tracked sale history; live mode checks each player against the real marketplace one at a time (slower — roughly 0.5–1.5s per player — but genuinely current). If your CSV has a value/price column, it's shown alongside the live floor so you can see at a glance whether your own valuation still holds up against where the market actually sits today. To include a club in the estimate, add a line like CLUB: Club Name — clubs don't have automated pricing yet (we're only just starting to track club sale history), so you'll enter its value manually, but it'll count toward the total bid.

Club squad requirements

Set how many clubs you have at each division tier, and their minimum player rating requirement. This drives the eligibility count below — computed live off your current holdings, so it updates automatically the moment a player sells.
Every club also needs 1 goalkeeper minimum, tracked separately from outfield players. Flag weighting: no flag / yellow / orange all count as 1 full player; a red flag counts as 0.5, since they're close to retiring.

Paste your data below, first row as headers. Works with a plain spreadsheet or a direct export from MFL itself.
Recognized columns (any order, only name and buy price are required):
name / player id / position / buy price / buy date / sold (yes/no) / sale price / sale date / batch (agency/group name)
Sale price should be the gross/listed price — MFL's 5% fee is deducted automatically when calculating P/L.
MFL exports work directly — firstName/lastName combine into name, id maps to player id, positions maps to position, Value maps to buy price (not Price, which is a valuation, not cost), and joinedAgency maps to buy date. Rows with no Value logged (e.g. bulk/block-bought players) get skipped — add those as one lump entry yourself if you know the block price.
